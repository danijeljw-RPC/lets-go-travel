# Australian Customer Terms and Notice Requirements

## Required documents

| Document | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Website/app Terms of Use | Account, acceptable use, intellectual property, platform availability and general rules. |
| Booking Terms and Conditions | Contracting role, supplier terms, pricing, confirmation, changes, cancellations, refunds, fees and servicing. |
| Privacy Policy | APP 1 policy and broader privacy transparency. |
| Collection Notices | Contextual APP 5 notices for account, traveller, booking, payment, minors and sensitive data. |
| Cookie/Analytics Notice | Cookies, analytics, session replay, preferences and controls. |
| Payment and Refund Notice | Merchant, descriptor, payment state, booking failure, refunds, disputes and chargebacks. |
| Minor Traveller Notice | Adult purchaser, guardian authority, documents, unaccompanied minors and privacy. |
| Complaints Policy | Intake, handling, escalation, response and external options. |
| Supplier Terms Presentation | Dated supplier terms/fare/rate rules accepted for the booking. |
| Accessibility Statement | Digital accessibility and assistance contact. |

## Booking terms clause schedule

### 1. Contracting entity

Include:

- full legal name;
- ABN;
- address;
- email/phone;
- trading name; and
- applicable licence/accreditation only where current.

### 2. Role

State the exact role per product:

- agent/intermediary;
- merchant of record;
- principal/reseller; or
- platform.

Explain who supplies the travel service and who is responsible for the platform's own service.

### 3. Supplier terms

- identify supplier terms;
- show them before purchase;
- retain the accepted version;
- explain order of precedence;
- do not incorporate unknown future terms without fair notice;
- do not state supplier terms remove ACL rights.

### 4. Offer and confirmation

Explain:

- search results are offers/availability subject to verification;
- price and availability may change before confirmation;
- payment is not confirmation;
- confirmation occurs only when the specified supplier confirmation is received;
- ticket issuance may be separate;
- what occurs if payment succeeds but booking fails; and
- duplicate booking prevention.

### 5. Price and currency

Explain:

- total price;
- breakdown;
- taxes/fees;
- local charges;
- platform fees;
- payment surcharges;
- currency;
- conversion;
- optional extras;
- repricing; and
- customer acceptance.

### 6. Payment

Identify:

- merchant of record;
- processor;
- statement descriptor;
- authorisation/capture;
- 3-D Secure;
- failed/abandoned payment;
- payment/booking mismatch;
- fraud review;
- settlement; and
- PCI/payment privacy boundary.

### 7. Changes and cancellations

Distinguish:

- customer voluntary change;
- supplier change;
- platform error;
- force majeure/third-party prevention;
- schedule change;
- property relocation;
- fare/rate rules;
- platform servicing fee;
- supplier fee;
- price difference; and
- approval process.

### 8. Refunds

Define:

- eligibility;
- request;
- supplier approval;
- platform processing;
- payment-provider processing;
- estimated time without unsupported guarantee;
- partial refund;
- non-refundable components;
- ACL rights;
- chargebacks;
- failed refund; and
- final settlement evidence.

### 9. Customer responsibilities

Include reasonable requirements for:

- accurate traveller details;
- passport/visa/entry;
- health/vaccination requirements;
- check-in;
- supplier rules;
- travel advice;
- insurance;
- connecting travel;
- minor authority; and
- prompt review of confirmation.

Do not use customer responsibility clauses to exclude platform obligations.

### 10. Flight servicing

State:

- booking retrieval is not live flight tracking;
- customer should check operating carrier for immediate operational status;
- exchange/refund/change support is capability-gated;
- manual escalation may be required;
- supplier response time is not guaranteed unless contractually committed;
- emergency airline contact may be necessary; and
- consumer rights remain.

### 11. Hotels

State:

- occupancy/age rules;
- check-in documents;
- local deposits/incidental cards;
- property-paid fees;
- special requests not guaranteed;
- relocation;
- local taxes; and
- cancellation time zone.

### 12. Minors

Include the approved requirements in [06-minor-travellers.md](06-minor-travellers.md).

### 13. Privacy

Link the privacy policy and collection notice, and explain supplier/overseas disclosures necessary to provide the booking.

### 14. Liability and ACL

Include a prominent savings clause:

> Nothing in these terms excludes, restricts or modifies any consumer guarantee, right or remedy that cannot lawfully be excluded, restricted or modified under the Australian Consumer Law or other applicable law.

Counsel must review all limitations, exclusions, indemnities and consequential-loss clauses for ACL and unfair-contract-term risk.

### 15. Complaints and disputes

Include:

- internal complaint channel;
- information required;
- response process;
- escalation;
- state/territory consumer agencies;
- governing law/jurisdiction; and
- no restriction on statutory rights.

## Terms presentation

- Present material terms before payment.
- Highlight cancellation/refund and merchant information.
- Require affirmative acceptance.
- Record version, timestamp, locale and booking.
- Provide downloadable/copyable terms.
- Use plain English.
- Avoid hidden hyperlinks for material fees.
- Provide accessible formatting.
- Re-obtain acceptance after a material reprice or term change.

## Unfair-term drafting rules

Avoid:

- unilateral unfettered discretion;
- permanent waiver of all refunds;
- keeping all money regardless of failure;
- broad platform immunity;
- one-sided termination;
- customer indemnity for platform fault;
- conclusive evidence clauses;
- foreign venue designed to deter claims;
- automatic acceptance of future terms;
- restrictions on reviews;
- blanket chargeback prohibition; and
- exclusion of all representations made during sale.

## Privacy policy schedule

See [03-privacy-cross-border-and-data-residency.md](03-privacy-cross-border-and-data-residency.md).

The policy must not claim:

- `we never share data` where suppliers receive it;
- `all data stays in Australia` where LiteAPI or suppliers process overseas;
- `we do not collect sensitive information` where accessibility/health data may be collected;
- `we delete immediately` where legal retention applies; or
- `children cannot use the service` where adult purchasers can book for children.

## Approval

Final documents require:

- Product factual verification;
- Finance fee/tax verification;
- Supplier Integration confirmation of supplier flows;
- Privacy approval;
- Security confirmation;
- Operations servicing confirmation; and
- Australian Legal/Compliance sign-off.

No generated template or overseas supplier term is production-ready without this review.
