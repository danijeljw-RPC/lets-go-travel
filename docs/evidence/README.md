# Evidence Register

Evidence records preserve dated supplier, regulatory and account findings that support canonical ADRs, open issues and production gates. They do not replace the controlling decision record or executed supplier agreement.

- [LiteAPI evidence](liteapi/README.md)
- [Australian market legal and compliance pack](../australian-market-legal-pack/00-README.md)

Evidence files may contain unresolved production checks even when the associated architecture decision is closed. The [Remaining Review Register](../decisions/review-register.md) states whether an item blocks implementation or only production activation.
