# Source Register

## Source-use rule

Primary legislation and regulator guidance are preferred. Industry material is clearly marked and must not replace legal advice.

The source register records the public position available on 29 July 2026. Laws and regulator guidance can change.

## Australian Consumer Law and pricing

### ACL-01 — ACCC: Price displays

- Link: https://www.accc.gov.au/business/pricing/price-displays
- Proposition: businesses must display the minimum total price as a single figure, including taxes, duties and unavoidable or pre-selected fees; a holiday price includes GST and airport taxes.
- Authority: Australian regulator guidance.

### ACL-02 — ACCC: Travel delays and cancellations

- Link: https://www.accc.gov.au/consumers/specific-products-and-activities/travel-delays-and-cancellations
- Proposition: consumer guarantees apply to travel booked directly or through an agent/platform; remedies can include replacement or refund depending on circumstances; supplier policies do not replace consumer guarantees.
- Authority: Australian regulator guidance.

### ACL-03 — ACCC: Consumer rights and guarantees

- Link: https://www.accc.gov.au/consumers/buying-products-and-services/consumer-rights-and-guarantees
- Proposition: consumer guarantees are automatic and cannot be removed by business wording.
- Authority: Australian regulator guidance.

### ACL-04 — ACCC: Card surcharges

- Link: https://www.accc.gov.au/consumers/pricing/card-surcharges
- Proposition: surcharge rules and minimum-price treatment where no surcharge-free method exists.
- Authority: Australian regulator guidance.

### ACL-05 — ACCC: Contracts and unfair contract terms

- Link: https://www.accc.gov.au/business/selling-products-and-services/contracts
- Proposition: from 9 November 2023, proposing, using or relying on unfair terms in standard-form consumer/small-business contracts is prohibited and penalties apply.
- Authority: Australian regulator guidance.

### ACL-06 — Competition and Consumer Act 2010 / Australian Consumer Law

- Link: https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A00109/latest
- Proposition: primary legislation, including single-price, misleading conduct, consumer guarantee and unfair-contract-term provisions.
- Authority: Commonwealth legislation.

### ACL-07 — ACCC: Travel and airports

- Link: https://www.accc.gov.au/by-industry/travel-and-airports
- Proposition: regulator travel-sector guidance and enforcement context.
- Authority: Australian regulator guidance.

### ACL-08 — ACCC: Webjet alleged misleading pricing and confirmation conduct

- Link: https://www.accc.gov.au/media-release/webjet-alleged-to-have-made-misleading-claims-about-airfare-prices-and-flight-bookings
- Proposition: specific regulatory warning about price representations and telling customers a booking was complete when it had not been booked with the airline.
- Authority: ACCC allegation/proceeding, not a final finding unless the proceeding records one.

### ACL-09 — ACCC: Jetstar and Virgin drip-pricing finding

- Link: https://www.accc.gov.au/media-release/court-finds-that-jetstar-and-virgin-australia-engaged-in-misleading-drip-pricing-practices
- Proposition: court findings concerning misleading drip-pricing practices.
- Authority: ACCC report of court outcome.

- Authority: State regulator guidance.

- Link: https://www.consumer.vic.gov.au/consumers-and-businesses/products-and-services/problem-with-a-service/booking-travel

## Minors and travel documents

### MINOR-01 — Australian Passport Office: Parental consent

- Link: https://www.passports.gov.au/consent
- Proposition: full parental consent generally means each person with parental responsibility consents to issue of the child's passport, subject to exceptions/processes.
- Authority: Australian Government.

- Link: https://www.passports.gov.au/help/child-alerts

- Authority: Australian Government.

### MINOR-03 — Australian Passport Office: Child passport

- Proposition: child passport and court-order pathways.
- Authority: Australian Government.

## Research limitations

- Public regulator guidance is not advice on the project's facts.
- A regulator page for Victoria is evidence of the Victorian licensing position, not a substitute for a national jurisdiction-by-jurisdiction opinion.
- Supplier pages are not executed account terms.
- ATAS material is an industry scheme.
- Airline, hotel and payment-method rules are product/account/date specific.
- The Children's Online Privacy Code was not final as at 29 July 2026.
